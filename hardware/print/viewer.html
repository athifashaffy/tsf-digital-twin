Buy before printing

  • 20 × 20 mm square bar (aluminium tube or a wooden strip), about 7 cm longer than your tank's width. The bar size is the BAR constant in the script.
  • 5 × PG7 cable glands (12.5 mm thread).
  • M3 screws: 4 × 10 mm (lid), 4 × 6 mm (Arduino), 4 × 12 mm with nuts (LCD), 4 × 10 mm with nuts (box to saddle).
  • M4 screws: 1 × 40 mm bolt with nut (saddle), 3 × 10 mm (thumb screws for the clamps and sonar holder), 2 × wood screws (cradle).
  • Zip ties up to 2.5 mm wide, and some silicone sealant for around the lid if water splashes the box.

Print settings

  • PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% infill, no supports. PLA works if the parts stay dry.
  • The box base takes about 6–7 h and the lid about 2 h on a typical 0.4 mm nozzle printer. All parts fit a 220 × 220 mm bed.
  • Print one rim clamp first and test it on your bar. If it's too tight or too loose, change FIT (default 0.6 mm) and regenerate.
